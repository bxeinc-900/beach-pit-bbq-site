Polished Concrete and Epoxy Flooring

And if you’re in the marketing to have your garage floor finished, either with polished concrete or epoxy Flooring, then give the guys over at South Coast Epoxy Flooring a call.

If you are looking for a new flooring option for your home or business, you may have come across polished concrete or epoxy flooring. But what exactly are these types of flooring? Here is a brief overview of polished concrete and epoxy floors to help you decide if either one is right for your space.

Polished concrete is created by grinding down the existing concrete until it reaches the desired level of shine. The concrete can then be stained or dyed to create a unique look. Epoxy floors are made up of a liquid resin that is poured over the surface of the concrete. The epoxy bonds with the concrete and hardens to create a durable, glossy flooring option. Both types of flooring are beautiful and can add a designer look to your space.
